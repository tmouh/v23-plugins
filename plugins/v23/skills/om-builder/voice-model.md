# V23 Voice Model

---

## SECTION 1 — GENERATION-PRIORITY RULES

These outrank stylistic completeness. Apply before and above every other rule in this file. Round-1 reproduction test showed that passages violating these rules were caught at 9/12; passages using them fooled all three judges.

**GP-1. Every claim block carries one computed downside anchor.**
Not asserted — computed: a rent hurdle vs. in-place ("$X/SF = full repayment, $Y below current in-place"), a fallback-use value floor, a basis floor vs. replacement, a coverage cushion. The four round-1 passages that stacked affirmative evidence without this device were all caught; both passages that used it fooled all three judges. This is the V23 fingerprint.

**GP-2. Underwrite below the validated comp, and show both numbers.**
Achieved $185/mo → underwritten $165/mo. The deliberate haircut between evidence and assumption reads as "traceable to a real model." Never let the underwritten number equal the comp.

**GP-3. Number texture: odd-precision + anchored.**
Model-fallout precision ($13,462/unit, 8.19%, 45.6%) beats round impressive figures (14.3%/yr, 8.2M SF). Macro stats require a named source or an as-of anchor in or near the sentence. If neither is available, cut the stat rather than float it. This matches the firm's verify-before-LP review culture (see Ambiguity Resolution c below).

**GP-4. Withhold. Leave asymmetry.**
Do not cover every standard OM category in sequence; do not close every clause; do not end blocks with verdict sentences ("...confirming both X and Y"). Highlight bullets stay clipped — evidence without self-explanation. One slightly rough construction per passage is healthier than uniform polish (see Ambiguity Resolution b below).

**GP-5. Every block, including descriptive asset-overview blocks, carries at least one defensive numeric or anchored comparison.**
Round-2 residual tell: the only passages still caught by 2 of 3 judges after GP-1–4 were applied were descriptive blocks with no protection calculus — judged "stylistically inert relative to authenticated passages, which always embed a defensive numeric." A location paragraph, a sponsor-track-record paragraph, and an asset-description paragraph each need at least one computed anchor (a basis comparison, a replacement-cost delta, a coverage ratio, a prior-deal benchmark) before they leave draft.

## SECTION 2 — VALIDATED VOICE RULES

11 rules, each with register scope and 2–3 cited corpus fragments. Hard bar: ≥3 materials spanning ≥2 years and ≥2 deals.

---

**V-01 — Open with a retained-by sentence, not a thesis sentence.**
Every investor-facing OM (deck or Word) names the agent, the sponsor, the capital type, and the size ask in that order as sentence 1. The thesis comes second. Never open with a market claim or an assertion about the deal's attractiveness.
_Register:_ investor deck OM, Word OM, outreach memo.
- "Vanadium Realty LLC ('Vanadium') has been exclusively retained by Greystone Capital LLC ('Sponsor') to structure and arrange preferred equity financing… The Sponsor is seeking approximately $14.3mm in preferred equity financing." — Wind Gap Pref OM final (E48), 2025-03-10
- "Vanadium Realty ('Agent') has been exclusively engaged by North Park Ventures ('NPV') to secure a programmatic LP equity partner of up to $50MM…" — NPV Florida IOS OM final (Slice D), 2026-06-09
- "Vanadium Group LLC ('Vanadium') is pleased to present to Global Asset Capital ('GAC') the opportunity to form a joint venture with USH…" — V23 Simple Presentation (Slice H), 2022-04-11

---

**V-02 — Investment Highlights bullet construction: Bold label – en/em-dash – evidence clause. No periods.**
Every bullet opens with a bold 2–4-word topic label, then a dash, then a plain-weight clause with the specific metric or evidence. No period. The bold label is scannable alone; the clause proves it.
_Register:_ investor deck OM (em-dash), Word OM (en-dash).
- "**Compelling Financials** – Project-level levered IRR >24.00% and EM of >2.00x; stabilized yield-on-cost of 8.19%" — 105 N13 final OM (Slice B), 2026-04-22
- "**Compelling Metrics** / The project underwrites to a levered IRR of 24.86% and an equity multiple of 1.76x over a 36-month hold, with a 6.88% untrended yield-on-cost." — Wind Gap Pref OM final (E48), 2025-03-10
- "**Proven Sponsor Capability** – Sponsor recently executed this strategy at a similar Brooklyn asset, achieving 38k SF leasing activity in 10 mo." — 105 N13 final OM (Slice B), 2026-04-22

---

**V-03 — Superlatives must anchor to a specific verifiable claim. Never free-float.**
Words like "incredible," "dominant," "strongest," "best," and "rare" are permitted only when attached to a named metric, date-ranged stat, or verifiable market fact in the same sentence. A free-floating superlative is a draft voice marker.
_Register:_ investor deck OM, Word OM (both; draft voice is the negative example).
- "the Lehigh Valley industrial market has exhibited an **incredible 19.9% rent growth** each year over the four-year period between 2020 and 2023" — Wind Gap Pref OM (E13/E43/E48), 2024–2025
- "The Sponsor is the **dominant** owner, operator and developer in the market with a **40+ year** track record" — Wind Gap Pref OM (E43), 2025-02-18
- Negative control: "We have the **best team in the nation** with the **most amazing new strategy**" — Obsidian PP (Slice A), 2025-11-30; excised before any investor send

---

**V-04 — In Word OMs, "Sponsorship" is the collective noun for all mid-sentence action references.**
When the Word OM describes an action the sponsor entity is taking ("is finalizing," "has been in contract," "is seeking"), use "Sponsorship" as the acting noun in mid-sentence references. Reserve "the Sponsor" for standalone noun phrases at section openings.
_Register:_ Word OM only (deck OMs use "Sponsor" throughout; this is a Word-OM-only rule).
- "Sponsorship has been in contract on this site since 2021, during which time Sponsorship has gotten the site fully entitled" — Wind Gap vSend 2 (E13), 2024-06-19
- "Sponsorship is finalizing an agreement with a parent rated BBB+ credit rating, build-to-suit tenant" — Wind Gap Pref OM (E43), 2025-02-18
- Same usage confirmed in Palmer MF OM (E68), 2025-01-30 (different asset class)

---

**V-05 — Downside protection is quantified as a rent hurdle (or equivalent unit-level threshold), not as a generic "margin of safety" phrase.**
In Word OMs for development deals, state downside protection as a specific dollar-per-unit or dollar-per-SF threshold at which the investor's principal is returned. Name it "rent hurdle" for industrial/office or the equivalent unit metric. Put this number in the Executive Summary.
_Register:_ Word OM (primary); deck equivalent is a milestone gate statement in Investment Highlights.
- "the rent hurdle for the remaining spec building for full principal repayment to the equity is only $3.25/SF" — Wind Gap Pref OM final (E48), 2025-03-10
- "the rent hurdle for the remaining spec building for full principal repayment to the preferred equity is only $4.29/SF" — Wind Gap July 2024 PDF (E35), 2024-07-24
- "To the Preferred Equity, the resulting metrics provide an extremely compelling basis of $141/SF, or **70% of terminal value**" — Wind Gap Exec Summary standalone (E60), 2024-07-02

---

**V-06 — The Word OM exec summary follows a strict five-beat order.**
Beat 1: retained-by sentence. Beat 2: S&U table. Beat 3: returns paragraph (IRR, EM, YoC, development spread). Beat 4: market thesis paragraph (opens with anchor stat). Beat 5: sponsor proof point sentence. This order is inviolable across capital types.
_Register:_ Word OM exclusively; deck exec summary uses dual-panel or 2×2 block pattern (see Section 4).
- Wind Gap Pref OM (E48, 2025-03-10): retained-by → S&U → "24.86% levered IRR and 1.76x EM… 6.88% YoC resulting in development spread of 138bps" → "incredible 19.9% rent growth" → "Sponsor was early to recognize the value and appeal of the Wind Gap sub-market"
- Wind Gap JV OM (E52, 2025-02-10): same five-beat, metric paragraph swaps to "~38% levered IRR and 2.29x EM… 7.54% YoC resulting in development spread of 204bps"
- Palmer MF OM (E68, 2025-01-30): same five-beat structure confirmed across different asset class

---

**V-07 — Incentive layers are quantified separately from base NOI/returns and framed as additive protection, never as the investment thesis.**
Show the base-case return without the incentive first, then show the incentive as a second line. Never lead with the incentive as the headline return metric.
_Register:_ investor deck OM, Word OM, strategy pitch; applies across all asset classes.
- "~$733k/yr [ICAP] savings; ~$10+/SF; 1.5x remaining OpEx" — separate "Tax Incentive" bullet after all financial return bullets in 105 N13 Investment Highlights (Slice B), 2026-04-22
- "NOI before Oklahoma Tourism Incentive: $1.9M (29% margin)" followed by stabilized NOI including incentive: $2.9M (45% margin) — Alley North Dev Analysis (Slice F), 2026-04-23
- "any favorable tax treatment viewed as an additional benefit rather than a primary driver of value" — Alpha Square / Open House Strategy Intro (Slice F), 2026-05-06

---

**V-08 — Three-tier slide header for investor deck OMs: ALL-CAPS eyebrow / sentence-case title / optional lead sentence.**
Every content slide uses: (1) ALL-CAPS section or status eyebrow at y≈0.50–0.55"; (2) sentence-case or title-case assertive slide title at y≈0.72–0.78"; (3) optional single lead sentence at y≈1.15–1.35" that supports or qualifies the title. No fourth tier.
_Register:_ investor deck OM, teaser; Word OMs use section-header paragraph style instead.
- Eyebrow "NORTH PARK VENTURES: SELECTED FIRM-WIDE PRIOR ACQUISITIONS" / Title "Sponsor Capability Across Geographies and Asset Classes" — NPV IOS final (Slice D sl.11), 2026-06-09
- Status eyebrow "RECENT CLOSING" above tile header — NPV IOS final (Slice D sl.13), 2026-06-09
- Section eyebrow "INVESTMENT HIGHLIGHTS" above bullet content — 105 N13 (Slice B), 2026

---

**V-09 — "Bottom line:" and "Translation:" are internal-only markers. Never in investor-facing materials.**
Internal analysis memos may use "Bottom line:" as a final-paragraph compression device and "Translation:" as a parenthetical interpretation of a sponsor claim. Their presence in a draft means that draft is not investor-ready.
_Register:_ internal analysis memo ONLY; negative space for all investor-facing registers.
- "Bottom line: This is a genuinely irreplaceable piece of real estate carrying an impaired operating story…" — W Aspen Investment Analysis Memo (Slice G G-48), 2026-06-11
- "Translation: Blue Harbor has zero completed automated dry-stack marinas as a sponsor…" — Blue Harbor JAX Internal Investment Memo (Slice G G-10), 2026-05-17
- Zero occurrences of "Translation:" or "Bottom line:" confirmed in any investor-facing OM across Slices B, C, D, E

---

**V-10 — Market thesis paragraph always leads with a named stat (number + period + source context), not a general claim.**
Open with the most compelling market stat stated as a bare fact: number first, then the what and where, then the source or time window. Do not open with "the market is strong" or "demand is growing."
_Register:_ investor deck OM, Word OM, teaser (stat block form).
- "the Lehigh Valley industrial market has exhibited an incredible 19.9% rent growth each year over the four-year period between 2020 and 2023" — Wind Gap all versions (E13, E43, E48), 2024–2025
- "Orlando is the lowest-vacancy IOS market in the country at 2.5% (Newmark, 2025), and Central Florida IOS rents grew +130.9% from 2020–2025 versus +123% nationally" — NPV IOS final (Slice D sl.8), 2026-06-09
- "Q3 & Q4 2025 saw a substantial 22% YoY uptick in Williamsburg office leasing" — 105 N13 final OM (Slice B), 2026-04-22

---

**V-11 — Platform-mode OMs suppress blended/aggregate return metrics in favor of per-deal + pipeline metrics.**
When an OM pitches a programmatic platform, the exec summary stat box carries per-deal target returns (IRR per deal, EM per deal) and a pipeline coverage figure — never a blended aggregate IRR. "Blended X% IRR" is a portfolio-mode metric that belongs in a deal-by-deal summary table, not the headline stat box.
_Register:_ investor deck OM (platform register only); does not apply to single-deal OMs.
- v0 (portfolio mode): "$39.2M / AGGREGATE COST BASIS" and "27.0% / BLENDED GROSS IRR" — NPV FL IOS v0 (Slice D), 2026-05-26
- Final (platform mode): "$50MM / LP EQUITY ASK" / "20%+ / TARGET GROSS IRR PER DEAL" / "$267MM+ / ACTIVE FL PIPELINE COVERAGE" — NPV IOS final (Slice D), 2026-06-09
- Build Blueprint explicitly prohibits re-introducing "blended 27% IRR headline" — NPV Build Blueprint.md (Slice D), 2026-06-04

## SECTION 3 — REGISTER VOICE MATRIX

Internal-only markers ("Bottom line:", "Translation:", "attractiveness rating X/10", "model delta demands scrutiny") are explicitly banned from all investor-facing output. Outreach memos are investor-facing; they receive no internal markers.

| Dimension | Investor Deck OM | Word OM | Teaser | Internal Analysis | Outreach Memo |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Opening move** | Retained-by on slide 2–3 (after cover + disclaimer) | Retained-by sentence 1 | Deal type + market gap on slide 1 | Deal at a Glance table | First-name salutation + one-sentence context |
| **Return framing** | KPI stat box (3–5 large numbers) on exec summary slide | S&U table + return paragraph in running prose | 1–2 KPI figures in eyebrow or closing slide | Sponsor returns vs V23 re-run, delta flagged | 4-cell KPI bar before prose |
| **Sponsor voice** | Third-person, credential-anchored bullets | "Sponsorship" collective noun for actions | Credential headline only (name + pedigree) | First-person V23 evaluation ("we reviewed," "in direct conversation") | "Our client" — V23 speaks on sponsor's behalf |
| **Risk treatment** | Absent from body; quantified downside protection in Investment Highlights bullet | Quantified rent hurdle / sensitivity table in Financial Analysis section; detailed risk quarantined to companion memos | No risk disclosure | Full SWOT + stress test + explicit "model delta" flagging | Not stated; returns presented as sponsor's |
| **Superlatives** | Anchored to a specific stat in same sentence | Anchored or absent | Manufactured urgency permitted ("The allocation is finite. The timing is now.") | Replaced by caveated assessment language ("moderately attractive with caveats") | "rare opportunity," "one of the most experienced" — factually grounded but warmer |
| **Internal-only markers** | NEVER: no "Translation:", "Bottom line:", "attractiveness rating," model-delta flags | NEVER | NEVER | Required: "Bottom line:", "Translation:", "attractiveness rating X/10," "model delta demands scrutiny" | None (outreach memos are investor-facing) |
| **Citation style** | Parenthetical superscript footnotes on exec summary stat box only; no per-slide sources in final versions | CoStar cited by name in market sections; no named sources elsewhere; no footnotes | Shortened source callout in tiny footer text | Sponsor attribution explicit; Tier 1 sources named inline | No citations |
| **Sentence length** | Short declarative; KPI values mid-sentence in bold | Full prose paragraphs; subordinate clauses permitted | Fragment-length labels + 1–2 sentences | Longer sentences with explicit qualifiers ("suggests," "consistent with") | Warm mid-length sentences; no bullet lists |

## SECTION 4 — HEADLINE GRAMMAR + EXEC-SUMMARY RECIPES

### Headline grammar by register

| Register | Construction |
|---|---|
| Investor deck OM (standard) | ALL-CAPS eyebrow + sentence-case assertive title |
| Investor deck OM (platform/GP) | Short declarative thesis sentence as hero text ("Powered land is bottlenecked by power and permitting. Obsidian solves both.") |
| Word OM section headers | ALL-CAPS chapter label; no subhead on most sections |
| Teaser | "THE X" two-word all-caps labels ("THE OPPORTUNITY," "THE MODEL," "THE TEAM") |
| Internal memo | Numbered sections ("1. Deal at a Glance", "4. Return Profile") |
| Outreach email/memo | No formal headers; runs as prose under salutation |

Investment Highlights bullet label pattern: `Bold noun phrase (2–4 words) + en/em-dash + plain evidence clause` — no period, Garamond 15.8pt (deck) or bold run (Word). Confirmed: 105 N13 all versions 2026-01 through 2026-04 (Slice B); Wind Gap all versions 2024–2025 (Slice E).

### Recipe A — Word OM Five-Beat Exec Summary
_Used in: Wind Gap Industrial (all versions 2024–2025), Palmer MF OM (2025), and all Word-format OMs in Slice E corpus._

1. **Beat 1 — Retained-by sentence:** `[Agent] has been exclusively retained by [Sponsor] to structure and arrange [capital type] financing for [asset description] located at [address]. The Sponsor is seeking approximately $[X] in [capital type] financing.`
2. **Beat 2 — S&U table:** Inline Sources & Uses with LP equity / GP equity / debt / total rows and $/GSF column. No prose around it.
3. **Beat 3 — Returns paragraph:** `The project underwrites to [unlevered IRR] and [unlevered EM], [levered IRR] and [levered EM] over a [X]-year hold, and [YoC] untrended yield on cost resulting in a development spread of [Xbps].` If pref register: add "As a result of the BTS tenant, the rent hurdle for full principal repayment is only $[X]/SF."
4. **Beat 4 — Market thesis paragraph:** Opens with the anchor stat (named number + period + time window + named source where available). Closes with submarket advantage sentence.
5. **Beat 5 — Sponsor proof point:** One sentence naming the sponsor's most specific proof of domain advantage (LogistiCenter founding, 315 Meserole 38k SF in 10 months, etc.).

_Registers: Word OM (pref equity, JV equity, equity partner). Replicates across capital types with metric swap only._

---

### Recipe B — Deck Dual-Panel Exec Summary
_Used in: 105 N13 all deck versions (2026), debt OM variant (2026-03-02)._

**Left panel:** 700–900-character narrative paragraph. Opens with retained-by sentence. Returns embedded in prose. Closes with market thesis.
**Right panel (two stacked tables):** (top) Uses & Sources (6–7 rows: LP equity / GP equity / debt / total, each with $$ and $/GSF); (bottom) Returns KPI table (3–4 rows: equity register → unlevered IRR/EM, levered IRR/EM, YoC/net profit; debt register → DSCR/debt yield/leverage).
**Slide 4:** Market and location narrative. Photo or map optional.

_The metric set in the right-panel KPI table is the primary register signal: equity → IRR/EM/YoC; debt → DSCR/debt yield/leverage._

---

### Recipe C — NPV 2×2 Named Block Layout
_Used in: NPV IOS OM 6/01 and final (2026). Platform register only._

Four named blocks at 2×2 grid (each ≈5.69" wide × 1.31" tall), each opening with an ALL-CAPS label + em-dash:
- **WHY NOW —** one sentence stating the institutional validation event (named deal or data point)
- **THE STRATEGY —** one sentence stating the sourcing/execution edge
- **THE PLATFORM —** one sentence on pipeline depth with a named coverage metric
- **THE ASK —** one sentence with target returns format (X%+ IRR / X.0x+ EM / hold period / roll-up framing)

Above the 2×2: subtitle line (e.g., "Programmatic Florida IOS Platform") + stat box with 5 KPI figures (LP ask / IRR target / EM target / pipeline / hold).

_Register: investor deck OM (programmatic platform only)._

---

### Recipe D — Krios/Co-invest Five-Row Card Layout
_Used in: V23 dark format Krios full OM (Slice C C-01), 2026-04-13._

Five gold-labeled rows (label left / body right):
- **The market gap:** one sentence naming the capacity shortfall with a specific number
- **The model:** one sentence on acquisition strategy (never build, never operate)
- **The proof point:** one sentence on precedent transaction
- **The platform:** one sentence on the infrastructure/operating partner
- **The access:** one sentence on why this co-invest is privileged access

_Register: V23 dark-format full OM for co-investment/co-sponsorship pitches._

## SECTION 5 — AMBIGUITY RESOLUTIONS

All legislated 2026-06-11.

**(a) LEGISLATED 2026-06-11 — GP-1 in Investment Highlights blocks.**
A basis floor with both numbers shown (e.g., "$141/SF basis vs. $195/SF replacement cost") satisfies GP-1 in an equity highlights block. A named rent hurdle is preferred where the register is pref equity or debt ("rent hurdle for full principal repayment is only $3.25/SF, $1.12 below current in-place"). Either form is compliant; the two-number basis floor is the minimum floor for the equity register.

**(b) LEGISLATED 2026-06-11 — "One rough construction" defined.**
The type specimen from the corpus: "After 6 bridge loans and a combination of various missteps and prolonged delays" (105 N 13, 2026) — slightly awkward but functional human syntax. This is not a typo or error; it is a deliberate register signal. One such construction per passage is the ceiling. Zero rough constructions reads as over-polished; two reads as careless. The test: it sounds like a principal talking, not like a redline.

**(c) LEGISLATED 2026-06-11 — GP-3 strict reading on macro/market stats.**
A macro or market stat requires a named source (time anchor alone is insufficient) or it gets cut from investor-facing prose. "19.9% rent growth each year over the four-year period between 2020 and 2023" is compliant because it carries a time window. "Orlando is the lowest-vacancy IOS market in the country at 2.5% (Newmark, 2025)" is compliant because it carries a named source. "The market has shown strong absorption" has neither and must be either sourced or cut. This matches the firm's verify-before-LP review culture and the cre-source-tiers.md standard.

**(d) LEGISLATED 2026-06-11 — Withholding vs 7–9-bullet completeness.**
In production Investment Highlights blocks, asymmetry lives within bullets (clipped evidence clauses, no verdict closers, varied construction) — not by thinning the section count. A 7–9-bullet Investment Highlights block is correct; a 4-bullet block thinned for "withholding effect" is wrong. The withholding is in how each bullet ends (no "confirming both X and Y"), not in how many bullets there are.

## SECTION 6 — STABLE VS. EVOLVING PATTERNS

### Stable (confirmed constant 2020/2022 → 2026)
- Retained-by opening sentence construction (earliest: V23 Simple Presentation 2020; latest: NPV IOS 2026-06-09)
- Investment Highlights bold label + dash + evidence clause (earliest: Wind Gap E13, 2024-06-19; latest: 105 N13 final, 2026-04-22)
- "Sponsorship" collective noun in Word OM action references (Wind Gap E13, 2024–2025; Palmer MF E68, 2025)
- Five-beat Word OM exec summary (Wind Gap E13, 2024 through Wind Gap E48, 2025)
- Anchor stat leading market thesis paragraph (Wind Gap E13, 2024 through NPV IOS, 2026-06-09)
- "Bottom line:" / "Translation:" as internal-only markers (confirmed absent from all investor-facing OMs)
- Incentive isolated as separate NOI line, framed as additive (Alpha Square/Open House, 2026-05-06; Alley North, 2026-04-23)

### Evolving (changed across the study window)
- Font system: Eras Light ITC (correspondence/internal, 2020–2024) vs Garamond (investor deck OMs, 2025–2026). Both current; register-differentiated, not contradictory.
- Source citation density: early deck OMs had per-slide source lines; final versions compress to parenthetical footnotes on exec summary stat box only.
- Disclaimer formality: trending toward more formal with institutional audiences.
- Deal-section structure: pre-2026 individual deal slides → mid-2026 tile slides with status eyebrows (RECENT CLOSING / ACTIVE PIPELINE / REALIZED CASE STUDY).
