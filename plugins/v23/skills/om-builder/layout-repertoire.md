LAYOUT SELECTION SYSTEM (READ FIRST — HOW TO CHOOSE, BEFORE THE CATALOG)
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Layout is not chosen from the raw content. It is chosen from the slide's
COMMUNICATIVE INTENT, and intent is recovered from the slide's ACTION TITLE
(the one-sentence takeaway). This is the consulting/Duarte/Zelazny consensus:
write the takeaway first, classify what kind of claim it makes, then pick the
arrangement that proves that specific claim. Picking a layout before you know
the takeaway is how decks end up template-repetitive.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
THE FOUR-STAGE SELECTION PROCEDURE (run for every slide)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

**Stage 0 — One-idea gate (MECE).** A slide carries exactly one idea. If the
takeaway needs an "and" that joins two unrelated claims, split into two slides.
One idea → one layout. (If you can't write a single takeaway sentence, the
slide isn't ready to lay out.)

**Stage 1 — Recover intent from the action title.** Read the slide's takeaway
sentence and classify it by its verb + relational operator into ONE intent
category (table below). The cue is grammatical:
- comparatives ("more/less/cheaper/outperforms/vs.") → Comparison
- partitives ("% of", "of total", "breaks down into", "composed of") → Composition
- temporal verbs/prepositions ("grew", "since", "by 2027", "up 123%") → Change-over-time
- sequence connectives ("from A→B→C", "then", "phases", "path to") → Process
- structural verbs ("sits above/below", "tiers", "rolls up to", "stack") → Hierarchy
- relational verbs ("depends on", "drivers of", "connects", "correlates") → Relationship
- locational ("located in", "at the intersection of", "within N miles", "submarket") → Spatial
- credibility framing ("our team", "track record", "N deals", "meet the sponsor") → Profile
- a single quantified assertion with no decomposition ("$X NOI", "once-in-a-cycle") → Big-idea
- convergence ("combine", "converge", "intersection of three forces") → Synthesis

**Stage 2 — Map intent → layout family.** Use the taxonomy table to pick the
layout family, then select the specific catalog layout (1–25) or hybridize per
the MANDATE. If the body is quantitative, nest the chart choice: Comparison→
bar/column, Composition→pie/stacked/waterfall, Change-over-time→line/column,
Distribution→histogram/scatter, Relationship→scatter/bubble (Zelazny/Abela).

**Stage 3 — Place elements per reading-flow.** Within the chosen layout:
- Action title at top, full-width, **punchline-first** (front-load the most
  informative words — the executive must get the slide from titles alone).
- ONE dominant anchor (the hero KPI, chart, or visual) on the primary optical
  path (upper-left to center). Everything else is visually subordinated
  (smaller, lower-contrast) so it never competes with the anchor.
- The "so what" / resolution lands where the eye exits: bottom-right for sparse
  Z/Gutenberg layouts, or directly under the anchor for hierarchy-driven ones.
- Absent a deliberate focal point, the eye defaults to an F-pattern and misses
  content — so always set an explicit anchor. (NN/g.)

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
CONTENT-ARCHETYPE TAXONOMY (intent → layout family → "use when")
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

| Intent | Layout family (catalog #) | Use when (action-title cue) | Anchor placement |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Comparison** | Two-/multi-column split, comparison table, bar chart (L8, L6, L19, L25) | "A outperforms B", "higher/lower than", "vs." | Parallel columns, equal weight; winner accented |
| **Composition / part-to-whole** | Segment: pie, 100% stacked bar, waterfall, twin-pillar (L18, L23) | "% of", "breaks down into", "composed of" | One chart center-left; dominant segment accented |
| **Change over time / trend** | Time-series line/column, dominant chart + callout (L7, L16) | "grew/fell N%", "since", "by 2027" | Chart fills primary area; endpoint/inflection annotated |
| **Process / sequence** | Flow: linear steps, timeline, swim-lane, Gantt (L22) | "from A→B→C", "phases", "path to close" | Left-to-right on reading gravity; critical step accented |
| **Hierarchy / structure** | Stack: tiers, pyramid, stacking plan, capital stack (L17, L18) | "sits above/below", "tiers", "rolls up to" | Top-down; apex/top tier as entry point |
| **Relationship / network** | Scatter, bubble, matrix, node-link (L19, L21) | "depends on", "drivers of", "correlates with" | Central hub or correlation cloud as focal point |
| **Geographic / spatial** | Map-anchored: locator + callouts, aerial, submarket map (L5, L13, L15, L21) | "located in", "submarket", "within N miles" | Map as hero (upper-left/center); pins + labeled callouts |
| **Profile / credibility** | Bio/profile grid, track-record tiles, KPI strip (L2, L4) | "our team", "track record", "N deals", "meet the sponsor" | Repeated parallel tiles (intentional repetition); name/number prominent |
| **Single big idea / assertion** | Hero: full-bleed image, one-big-number, KPI band on photo (L10, L14) | one claim, one number, no decomposition | One element dominates; everything else removed |
| **Synthesis / convergence** | Join: converging arrows, Venn, "comes together" | "combine", "intersection of three forces" | Convergence point centered as the resolution |
| **Narrative / story** | Single-narrative prose, sidebar + visual, narrative + chart (L1, L12, L13) | Executive Summary, Business Plan, Sponsor story | 2–4 idea-blocks; bold the load-bearing numerics |
| **Structured data lookup** | Table-dominant, twin-table (L6, L25) | Rent roll, comps, financials, pipeline | Right-aligned numerics; 5–7 columns max |

(A slide may carry a secondary intent — e.g., a Comparison slide whose body is a
table. Pick the layout by the PRIMARY intent of the action title, then realize
the body with the matching data-viz.)

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
VARIETY-VS-CONSISTENCY RULES (codified)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

**Hold constant across the whole deck (the consistency floor — these REPEAT,
that's what makes it read as one coherent deck):** master grid, type scale,
color/accent logic, margins, action-title position, page furniture (logo,
footer, page number).

**Vary deliberately (the variety engine — always vary the BODY arrangement):**
chart vs. diagram vs. image vs. table; one-column vs. two-column vs. quadrant;
left-anchored vs. full-bleed.

**The codified rules:**
1. **No-consecutive-repeat.** Never the same body layout on two consecutive
   content slides; ≤2 identical arrangements in any 4-slide window — UNLESS the
   content is a deliberate parallel series (e.g., four comparable deal exhibits,
   three sponsor case studies). In a parallel series, repetition IS the message
   and consistency is correct. Mark such series explicitly.
2. **One idea → one layout per slide** (MECE consequence).
3. **Section rhythm.** Open each major section with a higher-contrast
   "statement" layout (full-bleed or big-number), then settle into working
   layouts. This mirrors narrative tension/release.
4. **Anchor stability.** Even as arrangements change, keep the eye-entry point
   consistent (title top, primary anchor upper-left/center) so variety never
   costs scannability.
5. **Aim for ≥ 7 distinct arrangements across the deck.**

Repetition of layout is the single biggest tell that a deck was machine-built.
Content-weighted variety — every slide earning its own composition from its
intent — is the single biggest signal of editorial care.

════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
LAYOUT VARIETY MANDATE (READ FIRST — OVERRIDES THE 25-LAYOUT CATALOG BELOW)
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

The twenty-five layouts below are a starting library, not a ceiling. Treat every slide as a fresh design problem and invent the arrangement the information actually demands. Translate, rotate, resize, re-weight, invert, or hybridize any canonical layout, and reach for arrangements that aren't in the catalog at all:

- a tall portrait photo on the right two-fifths beside three stacked stat blocks left-aligned in the remaining three-fifths
- a horizontal photo strip across the top third with a four-column rent-roll table beneath
- a centered 40pt headline number flanked by two narrow vertical prose columns on either side
- a quadrant grid where each cell mixes one stat, one one-line caption, and one micro-chart
- a building elevation drawn as a tall column with floor-by-floor stacking-plan blocks fanning out to its right
- prose at top in a 2-1-3 weighted three-column rhythm above a half-height chart
- a single big number anchored hard to the right edge while supporting prose left-aligned eats the remaining two-thirds
- a navy header bar that runs only halfway across to make space for a chart bleeding into the title row
- a five-pin annotated map at 60% width with a vertical stack of comp cards filling the other 40%

**Aim for at least seven distinct arrangements across the deck. Never let two consecutive content slides share the same zone pattern.**

When the pre-written content (from Claude Code) suggests a layout in plain English ("large left photo, narrow right stat column, bottom caption strip"), use it as the starting point but improve where a smarter arrangement reads faster, hits harder, or sits more elegantly on the page.

Before composing each slide, ask: is there a smarter arrangement than the default that lets this particular set of facts read faster, hit harder, or sit more elegantly on the page? If yes, build the smarter one and document the deviation in one sentence at the end of the section.

Repetition of layout is the single biggest tell that a deck was machine-built. Thoughtful, content-weighted variety — every slide earning its own composition — is the single biggest signal of editorial care.

The layout choice for any given slide should be a direct reflection of the type of information that slide is conveying. The pre-written content provides the data; you provide the arrangement.

════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
THE 25 CANONICAL LAYOUTS (Starting Library)
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Pick or adapt the layout whose content type matches the slide's purpose. Default to the simpler arrangement when in doubt — a single-narrative slide is always safer than an over-designed one. Split slides freely if content fights for space; merge when ideas span thin slides. Hybridize freely per the MANDATE above.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
LAYOUT 1 — SINGLE-NARRATIVE (text-heavy)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

**Use when:** Executive Summary, Project Background, Business Plan narrative, Sponsor Background. Content is prose-driven — telling a story about the deal.

**Structure:**
- Full-width content area: x=60 to x=900, y=140 to y=480
- 2-4 idea-blocks of Garamond 13-14pt prose, each 2-4 lines
- 8pt blank paragraph spacer between idea-blocks
- Bold inline for key numerics and proper nouns only
- Optional: small KPI strip at the bottom (y=420-470) with 3-4 supporting stats — anchors the prose with concrete numbers

**DO:** make each idea-block a complete thought; bold the numbers that support the thesis; use 13-14pt body, not 11pt.

**DON'T:** wrap prose in cards or boxes; add icons before each idea-block; use bullets for narrative prose.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
LAYOUT 2 — KPI STRIP (stats-driven)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

**Use when:** Transaction Snapshot, Returns Summary, Asset Overview header. Content is a small set of key metrics that need to read instantly.

**Structure:**
- KPI strip occupies y=180 to y=320 (or y=140 to y=280 if no headline takeaway above)
- 3-5 equal-width columns from x=60 to x=900
- Column width = (840 − (N−1)×gap) / N, where gap = 12pt for 4-col, 8pt for 5-col
- Each column = pale-blue panel `#E8EEF6` fill, no border, no rounded corners
- Per column, three stacked elements (centered horizontally):
  - Top label: 9pt Arial all caps `#9AA3B2`
  - Big number: 26-36pt Aptos navy `#1F3A5F` bold
  - Sub-detail (optional): 10-11pt Arial italic gray `#485269`
- Below the strip: optional narrative explaining context, full-width 13-14pt Garamond

**DO:** equal column widths and equal gaps; center all three elements within each column.

**DON'T:** rounded corners on the panels; icons above the labels; mixed column widths.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
LAYOUT 3 — HALF-AND-HALF (image + text)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

**Use when:** Asset Overview, Adjacent Developments, Sponsor Track Record case study, Submarket Residential Overview. Content needs one strong visual anchor and parallel narrative.

**Structure:**
- Two zones at 50/50 split with a 20pt gutter:
  - Left zone (x=60 to x=470, y=140 to y=490): image, rendering, or chart
  - Right zone (x=490 to x=900, y=140 to y=490): narrative prose or stat list
- Image fills its zone or maintains aspect ratio centered; caption italic 8pt directly below image
- Right zone: 13-14pt Garamond prose; may include a small vertical KPI mini-strip (2-3 inline stats)

**DO:** keep image rectangular, hard edges (no rounded corners); caption directly under image; right zone narrative scans the image's content.

**DON'T:** wrap the image in a rounded frame; add icons in the right zone; make the two zones tell different stories.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
LAYOUT 4 — PHOTO GRID
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

**Use when:** Asset Overview — Photos, Neighborhood Amenities, Sponsor Track Record (multiple prior deals). Content is multiple visual elements with brief captions.

**Structure (pick by photo count):**
- 4 photos: 2×2, each ~410pt wide × 175pt tall
- 6 photos: 3×2, each ~270pt wide × 175pt tall
- 9 photos: 3×3, each ~270pt wide × 115pt tall
- Equal cell size and equal gutters (10-12pt)
- Each cell: hard-edge rectangle, photo fills cell
- Caption italic 8pt directly under each photo, inside the grid

**DO:** crop photos to consistent aspect ratio; equal cell sizes.

**DON'T:** round photo corners; float captions in separate boxes; mix photo aspect ratios within the grid.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
LAYOUT 5 — MAP-DOMINANT
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

**Use when:** Asset Location, Neighborhood Map, Adjacent Developments map. Content is geographic positioning where location IS the message.

**Structure:**
- Map fills ~70% of content area (x=60 to x=700, y=140 to y=490)
- Right zone (x=720 to x=900, y=140 to y=490): legend, pin descriptions, or context narrative
- Numbered pins ON the map, labeled inline next to each pin OR in the right legend with matching numbers
- Caption italic 8pt below map ("Source: Google Maps, V23 markup" or similar)

**DO:** annotate ON the map where possible; number-match pins to legend; show actual scale and orientation.

**DON'T:** float labels in slide corners; use icons for pins (numbered circles or chevrons are clearer); crop so tight the reader can't orient.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
LAYOUT 6 — TABLE-DOMINANT
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

**Use when:** Rent Roll, Comparable Sales, Comparable Leases, Operating Financials, Returns Summary detail. Content is structured data that needs to be scanned and compared.

**Structure:**
- Headline takeaway at top (Garamond 13-14pt italic, y=120)
- Table fills the rest: x=60 to x=900, y=160 to y=490
- Hairline top and bottom rules (0.75pt navy)
- Header row: navy fill `#1F3A5F`, white Aptos 11-12pt bold
- Thin rule under header row (0.5pt)
- No vertical lines, no interior horizontal lines (or very subtle 5% navy alternating shading for dense rows)
- First column (identifier): left-aligned
- Numeric columns: right-aligned
- Date / unit columns: center-aligned
- Source line italic 8pt at y=502

**DO:** right-align all numerics; use parens for negatives; abbreviate currency; keep column count to 5-7.

**DON'T:** vertical lines between columns; color-code rows by category; use icons in cells; wrap headers across multiple lines unless necessary.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
LAYOUT 7 — CHART-DOMINANT
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

**Use when:** NOI Build, Vacancy Trend, Returns Trajectory, Cash Flow waterfall. Content is a single chart that tells a quantitative story.

**Structure:**
- Headline takeaway at top
- Chart fills ~70% of slide: x=60 to x=900, y=160 to y=420
- Source line italic 8pt directly under the chart at y=440
- Optional brief narrative below chart: 1-2 lines, y=460-490

**DO:** label bars/lines directly; flat fills, navy primary `#1F3A5F`, mid-blue secondary `#5B7FA8`; horizontal-only gridlines sparingly.

**DON'T:** legend across the slide from the chart; 3D effects, gradients, drop shadows, chart borders; bevel or glow on data series.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
LAYOUT 8 — TWO-COLUMN COMPARISON
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

**Use when:** Base case vs. Stress case, Asset vs. Comp, Before vs. After. Content is a side-by-side comparison.

**Structure:**
- Two equal columns: x=60 to x=470 and x=490 to x=900, y=140 to y=490
- Each column has a header bar (navy fill, white Aptos 12-14pt bold, ~25pt tall)
- Below header: structured content (table, KPI list, prose paragraph) — same structure in both columns
- Equal alignment of comparable elements across columns

**DO:** identical row structures in both columns; highlight the delta with bold or sparing red on the worse side.

**DON'T:** different row counts across columns; different fonts or sizes; stack the two as full-width sections.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
LAYOUT 9 — INVESTMENT HIGHLIGHTS (bullet list with bold headers)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

**Use when:** Investment Highlights slide ONLY. This is a specific Vanadium pattern — NOT a card grid.

**Structure:**
- Headline takeaway at top
- 4-7 highlight blocks stacked vertically
- Each block:
  - **Bold Garamond navy** thesis statement (one line)
  - Body text below it: 13pt Garamond `#485269`, 2 lines explaining the thesis with bolded numerics
  - 8-10pt spacer to next block

**DO:** bold only the thesis statement line, not the whole block; sentence case in the thesis statement; keep each body to 2 lines max.

**DON'T:** card grids; icons before each highlight; numbered highlights; same opening word ("Strong," "Robust," "Compelling") on every highlight.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
LAYOUT 10 — FULL-BLEED VISUAL (rendering, floor plan, aerial)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

**Use when:** Building renderings, floor plans, large architectural diagrams, drone-shot aerials. Content is one large visual that needs to dominate.

**Structure:**
- Visual fills nearly the full slide: x=0 to x=960, y=80 to y=502 (extends past the standard margin)
- Minimal overlay: section eyebrow at y=38, section title at y=54 (visible against the visual, or with a small white overlay box if needed for legibility)
- Caption italic 8pt at y=502
- Footer bar unchanged

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
LAYOUT 11 — SECTION DIVIDER
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

**Use when:** Major section transitions. Optional — only if the deck is long enough to warrant breaks.

**Structure:**
- Mostly empty slide
- Section name in large Garamond ALL CAPS or small caps, navy `#1F3A5F`, centered both axes (or left-aligned at x=60, y=270)
- Thin horizontal rule below the section name, navy, ~200pt wide
- Footer bar unchanged
- No subtitle, no narrative

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
LAYOUT 12 — HYBRID: NARRATIVE + EMBEDDED CHART
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

**Use when:** Operating Financials, Returns Summary. Content needs both story-prose AND a quantitative anchor chart.

**Structure:**
- Top half (y=140 to y=300): narrative prose (Garamond 13-14pt, 2-3 idea-blocks)
- Bottom half (y=320 to y=480): chart spanning x=60 to x=900
- Source line italic 8pt at y=495 under the chart

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
LAYOUT 13 — SIDEBAR NARRATIVE + DOMINANT VISUAL
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

**Use when:** Carrying deal narrative on a page that also needs a strong visual proof — site map, aerial, rendering — without giving up prose density. Submarket Overview, Location Narrative, Adjacent Developments contextualized.

**Structure:**
- Left ~35% (x=60 to x=380): single column of body prose in a tinted hairline-bordered tile (very light blue panel `#F4F7FB`, 0.75pt black outline)
- Right ~65% (x=400 to x=960): one dominant image, map, or site plan that may bleed to the right and bottom edges
- Title bar across top 8-10% of slide above both zones

**DO:** anchor prose in a single 35%-wide column so the eye reads top-to-bottom before sweeping right to the visual; keep the prose-column edge rigid.

**DON'T:** let body copy wrap around or jog under the image; introduce a third column.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
LAYOUT 14 — KPI OVERLAY BAND ON HERO PHOTO
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

**Use when:** The "Offering" or "Price Card" slide — establishes price, cap rate, or yield with cinematic gravitas. Often the second slide of a deck after the cover.

**Structure:**
- Full-bleed photograph fills the entire 13.33" × 7.5" canvas
- Solid navy `#1F3A5F` horizontal band ~75pt (1.0") tall anchored at ~y=220 (~3.0") with 0.75pt black outline
- 2-4 oversized stat pairs inside the band: big number above (Garamond 36pt bold white), label below (Garamond 9pt all caps white), separated by 0.5pt vertical white hairlines
- Optional small prose paragraph in a 35%-wide column on the left, photo on the right of band

**DO:** use only 2-4 numbers — this is one statement, not a strip in disguise.

**DON'T:** fade or gradient the band; it must be a hard navy rectangle with crisp edges.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
LAYOUT 15 — ANNOTATED AERIAL WITH FLOATING CALLOUTS
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

**Use when:** The signature CRE location slide — shows the asset's position within a master plan, retail node, or office cluster, with surrounding occupiers labeled in context.

**Structure:**
- Aerial or oblique drone photo fills ~85-95% of slide (full-bleed or with a narrow top title bar)
- 6-12 floating tenant logos or place labels sit atop the photo, each anchored to its building position by a 0.5pt navy leader line or simply by placement
- Small demographic data table (3 columns × 3-4 rows: 1-mile / 3-mile / 5-mile radii) tucked into a bottom corner with 0.75pt black outline

**DO:** keep callout typography quiet — Garamond italic at modest size, hairline leader lines; logos drop-shadow-free.

**DON'T:** use bubble backgrounds, drop-shadowed pins, or colored circles behind logos.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
LAYOUT 16 — LEASE ROLLOVER CLIFF CHART
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

**Use when:** Communicating tenant rollover risk and WALT visually for multi-tenant office, industrial, or retail. Pairs naturally with the rent-roll slide.

**Structure:**
- Top 12% = section title + one-line action subtitle ("Staggered roll, no concentration through 2031")
- Middle 60% = vertical bar chart spanning the full slide width; x-axis = years (typically 10), y-axis = SF or % of NRA expiring, stacked by tenant or color-coded by credit tier (navy primary, mid-blue secondary, light-blue tertiary)
- Bottom 25% = horizontal hairline-divided table with 4-6 columns of summary metrics (WALT, % rolling Yr 1-3, top-5 tenant SF, etc.) with 0.75pt black outer border

**DO:** annotate any large rollover year with a small inline note ("Anchor renewal Q3 2027").

**DON'T:** 3-D bars, gradient fills, or rounded bar tops.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
LAYOUT 17 — STACKING PLAN (vertical floor diagram + sidebar)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

**Use when:** Office, mixed-use, or condo asset structure — who occupies what floor, by area and credit.

**Structure:**
- Left ~55% = vertical stack of horizontal rectangles, one per floor, scaled by floor SF, color-banded (light shades only — navy `#1F3A5F`, slate `#5B7FA8`, pale `#E8EEF6`) by tenant or status. Each rectangle 0.75pt black outline. Floor numbers run down the far left.
- Right ~40% = vertical hairline-divided table listing tenant, SF, % NRA, lease end, base rent

**DO:** preserve true SF proportionality between floor blocks.

**DON'T:** 3-D extrusion, isometric tilt, or shadows — flat 2-D only.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
LAYOUT 18 — SOURCES & USES TWIN-PILLAR BARS
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

**Use when:** Capital stack communication on equity-raise or recap decks. The canonical institutional rendering for sources/uses.

**Structure:**
- Two vertical stacked bars side-by-side, each ~150pt wide, centered in the slide
- Left bar = Sources (senior debt / mezz / preferred / sponsor / LP), stacked top-down, each segment 0.75pt black outline
- Right bar = Uses (acquisition / capex / closing costs / reserves / fees), same total height as Sources
- Each segment labeled to the right with $ amount AND % of total
- Right ~30% of slide = vertical legend + total $ summary

**DO:** label each segment with $ amount AND %; preserve total-height parity between the two bars.

**DON'T:** substitute stacked donut or pie charts — institutional readers expect twin-pillar columns.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
LAYOUT 19 — COMPARABLE SALES SCATTER (cap rate vs $/SF)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

**Use when:** Positioning the asset within recent trade activity. Visually defends the guidance.

**Structure:**
- Full-slide scatter plot occupying ~70% of slide (top 5% title + 7% bottom source/footnote band reserved)
- X-axis = $ PSF or $/unit; Y-axis = cap rate (optionally inverted so up = better)
- Each dot is a labeled comp (small Garamond italic), with subject as a navy filled square; comps as open circles
- Hairline rules form quadrants; a soft grey "guidance band" shades the implied pricing zone

**DO:** label only the 6-10 most relevant comps; cluster-label dense regions with a leader line.

**DON'T:** color-code by more than two attributes — readability dies.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
LAYOUT 20 — UNIT-MIX SPLIT (floor plan column + table column)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

**Use when:** Multifamily, condo, or office-suite mix presentation, where each unit type's plan and economics must be shown side-by-side.

**Structure:**
- Slide divides into 4 vertical bands of equal width (each ~3.3")
- Each band contains, top-to-bottom: small unit floor plan (top 50%, line-only architectural rendering), unit type label and SF (10%), rent / rent PSF / count mini table (40%, 0.75pt black outer border)
- Above all four bands, a single 8%-tall title bar

**DO:** render all four plans at the same scale so the eye can compare directly.

**DON'T:** round corners on the floor plans or add color washes — line-only architectural plans.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
LAYOUT 21 — SUBMARKET CYCLE QUADRANT
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

**Use when:** Positioning the asset's submarket within a 4-stage cycle (recovery / expansion / hypersupply / recession) without using a "Goldilocks" risk-return matrix.

**Structure:**
- Centered 5.5" × 5.5" square (0.75pt black outline) with two hairline axes dividing it into four labeled quadrants
- Subject submarket appears as a navy filled dot, with an arrow indicating direction of travel
- 4-8 peer markets shown as open circles
- Right ~25% of slide = brief narrative explanation of why subject sits where it does

**DO:** anchor with explicit, ungated quadrant labels at the four corners.

**DON'T:** compass-style rotation, diagonals, or animated arrows.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
LAYOUT 22 — BUSINESS PLAN WORKSTREAM TIMELINE (Gantt-style)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

**Use when:** The "execution map" — shows when each value-add initiative occurs across the hold. Common in IC memos and equity raise decks.

**Structure:**
- Left ~25% = vertical list of 5-8 workstreams ("Re-tenant 2nd floor," "Common-area renovation," "Refinance," "Disposition")
- Right ~75% = horizontal timeline grid with year columns (Yr 1 through Yr 5 or 7) and a horizontal bar per workstream stretching across the relevant period; each bar 0.75pt black outline
- Hairline vertical year guides; small diamond markers for milestones

**DO:** single-weight rectangles, not gradient or tapered bars.

**DON'T:** more than three bar colors (active / planned / milestone) — readability dies.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
LAYOUT 23 — NOI BRIDGE / WATERFALL COLUMN CHART
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

**Use when:** Walking the reader from in-place NOI to stabilized NOI by component (rent growth, lease-up, expense savings).

**Structure:**
- Top 10% title
- Middle 70% = horizontal sequence of floating columns: a left anchor column at "In-Place NOI", 4-7 floating positive/negative columns each annotated with $ value and label, ending in a right anchor column at "Stabilized NOI". Each column 0.75pt black outline.
- Connector dashed 0.5pt lines at each column top show running total
- Bottom 20% = optional sidebar of assumptions in fine-print Garamond

**DO:** label each delta column with both $ and the driver ("+$420k contractual escalations").

**DON'T:** red/green for positive/negative — use solid navy for positive, hairline-outlined for negative.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
LAYOUT 24 — RENOVATION BEFORE-AND-AFTER SPLIT
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

**Use when:** Communicating physical value-add — lobby, amenity, unit renovation evidence.

**Structure:**
- Single horizontal split, slide divided exactly 50/50 left-right
- Left photo = "current" condition with 0.75pt black outline
- Right photo = "renovated" rendering or comparable with 0.75pt black outline
- 0.5pt hairline vertical rule between them
- Small "BEFORE" / "AFTER" labels in upper-left of each photo (Garamond 9pt small caps)
- Bottom 12% = one-line narrative under each photo

**DO:** match camera angle, focal length, and crop ratio between the two images.

**DON'T:** stack vertically — institutional readers expect side-by-side.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
LAYOUT 25 — TWIN-TABLE FINANCIAL LAYOUT (property + rent schedule)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

**Use when:** Net-lease / single-tenant pages where readers need property facts and rent escalation schedule side-by-side without scrolling.

**Structure:**
- Top 8% title band
- Two tables of identical height occupy left ~45% and right ~45% of slide width, separated by a ~3% gutter; each table 0.75pt black outer border
- Left table = property facts (address, year built, SF, parcel, lease type) — 2 columns, label-left/value-right
- Right table = rent schedule (4-6 columns: start, end, annual, monthly, PSF, % increase)
- Below right table, a small inset photo (~25% of slide height) with 0.75pt black outline fills bottom right

**DO:** keep both tables to identical row heights so they read as a matched pair.

**DON'T:** different shading schemes between the two tables — one shading scheme only.
