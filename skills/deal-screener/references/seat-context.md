# Seat Context — V23's Four Lenses

The same OM gets a meaningfully different screening report depending on which seat V23 is in. This file documents the lens for each seat. Read the relevant section before producing the report.

## Seat 1 — Equity Capital Placement (sponsor-side)

V23 is mandated by a GP / operator to find LP equity for an acquisition or recap.

**Primary question:** Is this deal placeable to V23's LP base, and if so, at what terms?

The deal does NOT have to be one V23 would do as principal — V23's own underwriting standard is more stringent than what's placeable in the LP market. But it does have to be:
- Cleanly underwritten with defensible assumptions
- Backed by a sponsor with a credible track record OR a credible co-GP / KP arrangement
- Structured with reasonable LP economics (pref ≥7-8%, promote tiers reasonable, fees not punitive)
- In a market and asset class LPs are currently appetited for
- Of an equity check size that matches V23's LP relationships

**What to weight more heavily in the screen:**
- Sponsor track record narrative — is there a clean story to tell?
- Comparable deals the sponsor has done full-cycle, especially recent ones
- The promote structure and whether it's market
- The equity check size relative to typical LP appetite
- Whether the deal is "fundable" in the current LP market (mid-2026: institutional appetite is selective; family offices and HNW are the primary check-writers for many sponsors)
- Whether the asset class is currently in or out of favor with LPs (e.g., office is hard regardless of fundamentals)

**What to weight less:**
- Whether V23's principal arm would do the deal — that's a different bar
- Marginal underwriting nits that won't move the LP decision

**Specific screening questions in this seat:**
- Is the sponsor's track record packet adequate to send to LPs? If not, what's missing?
- Are returns at the LP level (after fees, promote, structure) attractive enough to compete for capital?
- What's the LP-level pref, and is it at-market?
- What's the sponsor's prior IRR on comparable deals — full-cycle, gross and net?
- Can V23 get conviction on this enough to lead conviction with LPs?

**Recommendation framing:** "Pursue / Pass / Conditional" should be expressed as "placeable / not placeable / placeable with these modifications."

## Seat 2 — Debt Capital Placement (sponsor-side)

V23 is mandated to source senior debt, mezz, or pref equity for a sponsor.

**Primary question:** What debt / mezz / pref is achievable, and does it close the capital stack at terms that work?

The deal underwriting is less the focus than the financeability:
- Is the asset financeable in the current debt market?
- What lender types make sense (banks, debt funds, life cos, agency for MF)?
- What's a market quote on senior at current LTV / debt yield / DSCR?
- Where does the gap need filling — mezz, pref, or additional sponsor equity?
- Does the basis support a clean capital stack?

**What to weight more heavily:**
- DSCR at the loan request, and at refinance maturity
- Debt yield (NOI / loan amount) — most lenders now underwriting to debt yield as primary metric
- Loan-to-value, loan-to-cost (for value-add or development)
- Cash management triggers and lender intercreditor terms
- Sponsor net worth and liquidity for guarantees / recourse carve-outs
- Refinance / take-out feasibility
- Property cash flow stability (more critical for senior than for equity)
- Asset class liquidity in the debt market (office is brutal; industrial and MF are easier)

**What to weight less:**
- LP-level returns — not V23's job in this seat
- GP promote structure — not V23's job in this seat

**Specific screening questions in this seat:**
- What's the senior loan request (amount, rate type, term, IO, amortization, recourse)?
- At current market rates, what's the DSCR / debt yield / LTV at the requested loan amount?
- Is there a gap to be filled with mezz / pref? At what cost?
- What's the refinance plan and does it pencil at +100bps stressed take-out rates?
- Sponsor liquidity / net worth for guarantee tests?
- Any lender hot-button issues with this asset (concentration, environmental, lease structure)?

**Recommendation framing:** "Financeable at $X loan amount with [structure]" or "Not financeable in current market without significant equity contribution."

## Seat 3 — Principal Investing (V23's own balance sheet)

V23 is evaluating the deal as a buyer on its own account.

**Primary question:** Is this a deal V23 would buy — at what price, with what conditions?

The most stringent lens. Apply every red flag in `references/red-flags.md` ruthlessly. Every assumption gets stress-tested. Every sponsor claim gets challenged.

**What to weight most heavily:**
- Basis vs replacement cost
- Quality of in-place cash flow (the foundation under any value-add story)
- Defensibility of pro forma assumptions
- Exit liquidity at projected terminal yield
- Risk-adjusted return relative to V23's hurdle (state if known; otherwise default to: 18%+ levered IRR, 1.7x+ MOIC, with sub-12% coupon downside protection for value-add; 12-15% IRR, 1.4x+ for core-plus; 20%+ for opportunistic)
- Specific items that would surface in V23's own due diligence (PCA, environmental, title, survey, market study)

**What to weight less:**
- Whether the OM is "well-marketed" — irrelevant
- Sponsor track record — V23 is the sponsor in this case

**Specific screening questions in this seat:**
- At what price (or range) would V23 transact?
- What's the highest-confidence base-case IRR / MOIC / cash-on-cash?
- What's the bear case at market-conservative assumptions?
- What's the must-have due diligence deliverable list to firm up the bid?
- What's the financing strategy?
- What's the exit thesis and is it credible?

**Recommendation framing:** "Pursue at $X with [conditions]" or "Pass — primary reason is __" or "Conditional — proceed to LOI at $X subject to [diligence outcomes]."

## Seat 4 — LP-Side Advisory

V23 is helping an institutional LP evaluate a deal that's been brought to them by a sponsor.

**Primary question:** Should the LP commit, and at what terms?

The lens is similar to principal in rigor, but with two LP-specific overlays:
- **Sponsor / GP risk is paramount.** The LP is buying a sponsor as much as buying a property. Sponsor due diligence dominates.
- **Structure / alignment / governance matter more.** The LP is structurally passive. Pref, promote, GP co-invest, governance rights, reporting, exit rights — these are the LP's main protections.

**What to weight most heavily:**
- Sponsor track record (depth, asset class, market, strategy match)
- LP-level economic alignment (pref, catch-up, promote, lookback / true-up)
- LP-level governance (ROFO/ROFR, major decision approvals, removal rights, key-person triggers)
- LP-level transparency (reporting cadence, audit rights, capital call mechanics)
- Risk-adjusted LP-level return (after fees, promote, taxes if relevant)
- Exit liquidity for the LP (sale, recap, secondary market)
- Concentration / portfolio fit for the LP

**What to weight less:**
- GP-level economics — relevant only insofar as alignment
- The marketing polish of the OM

**Specific screening questions in this seat:**
- What does the LP-level IRR / MOIC look like at base / bear / bull?
- Is the pref at market (≥8% accruing for value-add, ≥7% for core-plus)?
- Is there a true-up / lookback at exit?
- What governance / approval rights does the LP get?
- What's the GP co-invest amount and source (real cash vs deferred fees)?
- Has the sponsor delivered on prior funds at all? At target returns?
- What's the LP's recourse if the sponsor underperforms?

**Recommendation framing:** "Recommend commitment of $X" or "Recommend pass — primary reason ___" or "Conditional on negotiating [list]."

## When the seat is unclear

If the user invokes the skill without specifying the seat, ask one question at the start:

> "Quick check before I dive in — is V23 advising the sponsor (placing equity / debt) on this deal, looking at it as principal, or evaluating it on behalf of an LP client?"

If the user says "neutral" or "just screen it," default to the **principal** lens — it's the most demanding and produces the most thorough screen. Note in the output that the principal lens is being applied and that the report can be re-cast for a different seat if needed.

## When the seat is mixed

Sometimes V23 is doing both equity and debt placement on the same deal, or principal + LP advisory. In that case, produce one report with one V23 Seat & Engagement section that names the dual mandate, and weight the analysis accordingly. Don't write two separate reports.
